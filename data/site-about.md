Tom Vellavoor Saji

Senior AI Engineer — Azure AI, Agentic Systems, and Enterprise LLM Platforms

I've spent the last four years designing and shipping AI systems that run in production for some of the largest organisations in Qatar — spanning media, education, government, and investment. Before that I studied Electrical & Electronics Engineering, which is where I first got interested in the intersection of systems thinking and software.

I work best at the boundary between research and production: taking ideas from papers and prototypes and turning them into systems that are reliable, observable, and maintainable at scale. Right now I'm especially focused on agentic architectures — multi-agent orchestration, evaluation pipelines, and the infrastructure that makes agents trustworthy in real deployments.

Currently building AI infrastructure at scale across Qatar — spanning enterprise chatbots, real-time avatar platforms, multilingual media AI, and government-grade agentic systems.
